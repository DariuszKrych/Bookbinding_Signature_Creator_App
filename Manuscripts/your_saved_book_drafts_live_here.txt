Books you write in the app's "Convert Inputted Text into PDF Signatures" view
are saved here, one JSON file per draft, named after the draft.

Each file holds everything: the title page, every section's heading and text,
and the page size, margins and typeface it is set in. They are plain text, so
you can copy one to another machine, keep one in version control, or open one in
a text editor if you ever need to.

Nothing in here is read by the conversion side of the app. A draft becomes an
ordinary PDF in Input/ the moment you click "Create the book PDF", and takes the
same route as any other book from there.
